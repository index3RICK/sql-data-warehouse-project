# 📊 Advanced Analytics

Transforming business data into actionable insights using SQL analytical techniques on the Data Warehouse **Gold Layer**.

---

## 📌 Description

This module contains a collection of SQL queries designed to answer key business questions. The results obtained serve as the foundation for executive dashboards, customer analysis, product analysis, and data-driven decision-making processes.

The analyses developed in this layer were later integrated into **Power BI** for building interactive dashboards.

---

## 📈 Analytical Modules

### ⏳ 1. Trend Analysis

Analyzes business evolution over time.

* Monthly Sales
* Monthly Customers
* Quantity Sold
* Revenue Growth

---

### 📈 2. Cumulative Analysis

Calculates progressive business growth.

* Cumulative Sales
* Cumulative Performance
* Historical Revenue Evolution

---

### 🚀 3. Performance Analysis

Evaluates product and category performance.

* Year-over-Year Comparison
* Historical Average Comparison
* Performance Ranking
* Top Product Identification

---

### 🥧 4. Part-to-Whole Analysis

Determines how much each group contributes to the total business outcome.

* Sales by Category
* Percentage Share
* Product Segment Contribution

---

### 🎯 5. Data Segmentation

Classifies business entities using business rules.

#### 👥 Customer Segmentation

* 🟢 VIP
* 🔵 Regular
* 🟡 New

#### 📦 Product Segmentation

* 🟢 High Performer
* 🟡 Mid Range
* 🔴 Low Performer

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered

### Sales

* How have sales evolved over time?
* What is the cumulative business growth?
* What trends are observed in revenue?

### Customers

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers belong to each segment?
* Which customers have stopped purchasing recently?
* What is the average spending behavior?

### Products

* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which categories contribute most to the business?
* Which products show the best performance?
* Which products require attention?

The results of these queries were used to develop:

These dashboards enable the transformation of SQL analysis into interactive visualizations for decision-making.
