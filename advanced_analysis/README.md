# 📊 Advanced Analytics

Transforming business data into actionable insights using SQL analytical techniques on top of the **Gold Layer** of the Data Warehouse.

---

## 📌 Description

This module contains a collection of SQL queries designed to answer key business questions. The results generated serve as the foundation for executive dashboards, customer analytics, product analytics, and data-driven decision making.

The analytical outputs developed in this layer were later integrated into **Power BI** to create interactive dashboards.

---

## 📈 Analytics Modules

### ⏳ 1. Trend Analysis

Analyzes business evolution over time.

* Monthly Sales
* Monthly Customers
* Units Sold
* Revenue Growth

---

### 📈 2. Cumulative Analysis

Measures progressive business growth.

* Running Sales Totals
* Cumulative Revenue
* Historical Growth Trends

---

### 🚀 3. Performance Analysis

Evaluates product and category performance.

* Year-over-Year Comparison
* Historical Average Comparison
* Performance Classification
* Top Product Identification

---

### 🥧 4. Part-to-Whole Analysis

Determines how much each group contributes to the overall business.

* Sales by Category
* Percentage Contribution
* Product Segment Contribution

---

### 🎯 5. Data Segmentation

Classifies business entities using business rules.

#### 👥 Customer Segmentation

* 🟢 VIP
* 🔵 Regular
* 🟡 New

#### 📦 Product Segmentation

* 🟢 High Performer
* 🟡 Mid Range
* 🔴 Low Performer

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered

### Sales

* How have sales evolved over time?
* What is the cumulative growth of the business?
* What revenue trends can be identified?

### Customers

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers belong to each segment?
* Which customers have not purchased recently?
* What is the average spending behavior?

### Products

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which categories contribute the most to the business?
* Which products perform best?
* Which products require attention?

The outputs generated by these SQL analyses were used to build:

* 📈 Executive Dashboard

* 📦 Product Analytics Dashboard

These dashboards transform SQL-based analysis into interactive visual insights that support business decision-making.
