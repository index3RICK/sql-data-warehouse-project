# Advanced Analytics

This module contains business-oriented SQL analytics developed on top of the Gold Layer of the Data Warehouse.

The objective is to transform curated data into actionable business insights that support reporting, decision-making, and Business Intelligence dashboards.

---

## Analytics Categories

### 1. Changes Over Time Analysis

Analyzes how business metrics evolve across time periods.

Examples:

* Monthly Sales Trends
* Revenue Growth
* Customer Activity Trends
* Product Performance Trends

---

### 2. Cumulative Analysis

Measures running totals and cumulative business performance.

Examples:

* Cumulative Revenue
* Running Sales Totals
* Customer Growth Over Time

---

### 3. Performance Analysis

Evaluates business entities against key performance indicators.

Examples:

* Top Customers
* Top Products
* Revenue by Category
* Product Performance Ranking

---

### 4. Part-to-Whole Analysis

Measures contribution percentages and distribution across business dimensions.

Examples:

* Revenue by Product Category
* Revenue Contribution by Product Segment
* Customer Revenue Distribution

---

### 5. Segmentation Analysis

Groups business entities into meaningful segments for strategic analysis.

Examples:

* Customer Segmentation

  * VIP
  * Regular
  * New

* Product Segmentation

  * High Performer
  * Mid Range
  * Low Performer

---

## Business Questions Addressed

The SQL analytics layer was designed to answer questions such as:

### Sales

* How have sales evolved over time?
* What are the long-term sales trends?
* What is the cumulative revenue growth?

### Customers

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers belong to each segment?
* Which customers have not purchased recently?
* What is the average spending behavior?

### Products

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which categories contribute the most to sales?
* Which products are top performers?
* Which products require attention?

---

## Integration with Business Intelligence

The outputs generated by these analytical SQL queries were later integrated into Power BI dashboards to provide interactive reporting and business monitoring.

### Dashboards Built

* Executive Dashboard
* Customer Analytics Dashboard
* Product Analytics Dashboard

These dashboards transform raw analytical outputs into visual business insights that support strategic decision-making.
