A complete end-to-end Data Engineering and Business Intelligence solution built with PostgreSQL and Power BI following the Medallion Architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold).

This project demonstrates the full lifecycle of modern data analytics, including data ingestion, cleansing, transformation, dimensional modeling, business reporting, advanced analytics, and dashboard development.

---

# 🚀 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform raw CRM and ERP data into actionable business insights through a structured Data Warehouse and Business Intelligence environment.

The solution includes:

* Data ingestion from multiple source systems
* Data quality validation and cleansing
* Medallion Architecture implementation
* Dimensional modeling (Star Schema)
* Analytical reporting views
* Advanced SQL analytics
* Interactive Power BI dashboards
* Business performance monitoring

# 📊 Business Questions Answered

The analytical layer was designed to answer key business questions such as:

### Sales Performance

* How have sales evolved over time?
* What are the long-term sales trends?
* What is the cumulative revenue growth?

### Customer Analytics

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers belong to VIP, Regular, or New segments?
* Which customers have not purchased recently?
* What is the average customer spending behavior?

### Product Analytics

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which categories contribute the most to total sales?
* Which products are high performers?
* Which products require business attention?

### Business Insights

* What percentage of revenue comes from each category?
* How does product performance compare to historical averages?
* Which business segments drive profitability?
